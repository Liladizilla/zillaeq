Place your launcher icons and logo images here.

Recommended names and sizes (Android mipmap):
- mipmap-mdpi/ic_launcher.png  (48x48)
- mipmap-hdpi/ic_launcher.png  (72x72)
- mipmap-xhdpi/ic_launcher.png (96x96)
- mipmap-xxhdpi/ic_launcher.png (144x144)
- mipmap-xxxhdpi/ic_launcher.png (192x192)

Alternatively use the Flutter `flutter_launcher_icons` package to generate icons automatically.

To reference a logo in the app UI, place a PNG at assets/logo.png and ensure pubspec.yaml includes:

flutter:
  assets:
    - assets/logo.png
